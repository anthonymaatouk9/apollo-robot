Capabilities
Everything Apollo can do
Built for real hospital environments with safety, accuracy, and reliability at every step.

Autonomous navigation

LiDAR-based navigation with real-time obstacle detection and avoidance. Robot maps its environment and stops instantly if the path is blocked, resuming automatically when clear.

QR patient verification

Every delivery verified by live QR code scan. Wrong patient or wrong room — mission immediately aborted and nurse alerted.

4-tube dispensing

Stepper motor selects correct tube. Servo drops exactly one pill. IR sensor confirms drop before proceeding.

Automatic water & cup

Cup automatically dispensed and filled with controlled water amount — measured by flow sensor for precision.

AI intake verification

YOLOv8 + MediaPipe monitor the patient taking medication. Session recorded and saved as video evidence per mission.

Real-time dashboard

Full web dashboard — manage patients, schedule medications, track missions live, receive instant alerts from any device.

Battery & stock monitoring

Real-time battery tracking with automatic charging below 30%. Pill stock and water tank levels monitored with proactive alerts.

Role-based security

Admin-only account creation. Nurses cannot self-register. All actions logged with timestamps for full medical audit trail.

Questions
Frequently asked
Everything you need to know about how Apollo works.
How does Apollo verify the correct patient?
Apollo uses a unique QR code for each scheduled delivery. The QR encodes the patient ID, room, and schedule ID. When the robot arrives, it scans the QR with its live camera and verifies all three fields match before dispensing any medication. A mismatch immediately aborts the mission and alerts the nurse.
What happens if the robot encounters an obstacle?
Apollo has an ultrasonic sensor that continuously monitors the path ahead. If an object is detected within 30cm, the robot immediately stops and waits. Once the path is clear, it automatically resumes navigation. The nurse dashboard shows obstacle alerts in real time.
How does the AI confirm the patient took their pill?
After dispensing, Apollo activates its camera and uses YOLOv8 to detect the patient and cup while MediaPipe tracks hand movement. When the system detects the patient raising the cup toward their face in 3 consecutive frames, intake is confirmed. The entire session is recorded and saved as video evidence.
How many different medications can Apollo carry?
Apollo has 4 medication tubes, each loaded with a different pill. A stepper motor rotates to the correct tube for each patient's prescription. Each tube holds up to 30 pills and stock levels are tracked in real time on the dashboard with low-stock alerts.
Can nurses control Apollo from their phone?
Yes. The nurse dashboard is a web application accessible from any device on the hospital network — phone, tablet, or desktop. Nurses can schedule deliveries, monitor missions live, trigger manual dispenses, view live camera feeds, and receive instant alerts all from a browser.
What happens if the battery runs low during a mission?
Apollo monitors battery continuously. A warning alert appears on the dashboard below 30%. Below 20%, all new missions are blocked to prevent the robot from running out of power mid-delivery. The robot can be sent to charge manually from the dashboard or automatically when idle.
How are nurse accounts managed?
Only the admin account can create nurse accounts — nurses cannot self-register. The admin panel allows creating, deactivating, and resetting passwords for all accounts. All login activity and operations are logged with timestamps for a full audit trail.
